# Connected-domain Expansion — 2026-09-11

> Historical implementation record. See the
> [canonical S5.5 status matrix](../validation/s5-5-status-matrix.md) for current acceptance.

## Reconciled Checkpoint

- Apple Contacts, Location/ETA/Weather, derived Health and Screen Time gate packages were integrated
  into the iOS Runner. Available Apple and Android Personal Views publish through a strict expiring
  local FFI store; Screen Time remains typed unavailable without its entitlement.
- Schedule and seven expansion Experts gained production multi-View paths and durable enabled
  assignments whose advertised cards and reads follow person-scoped source grants.
- Runtime routing gained device presence/scope, freshness, transfer-class and explicit-disagreement
  enforcement. Cross-device transport remained S8-only.
- No qualifying signed physical-device, SaaS live-provider or integrated failure/privacy corpus was
  recorded. Formal S5.5 acceptance remained **0/14**.

## Provider Parity

- Google Calendar received a bounded read-only adapter, isolated PKCE credential, selected-calendar
  configuration, lifecycle inventory and authenticated product route.
- Microsoft Mail and Calendar received isolated least-privilege OAuth profiles, bounded provider-
  neutral projections, typed failure/cache handling and product routing.
- Microsoft Teams received selected-team/channel persistence, exact delegated read scope and a
  deterministic provider-neutral Work Context route.
- Android Calendar, Contacts and Health Connect gained public-provider adapters, explicit settings
  actions, bounded projections, lifecycle snapshots and strict local View publication.
- Evidence: [provider parity](../validation/s5-5-provider-parity-foundation.md),
  [Android context](../validation/s5-5-android-context-foundation.md), and
  [context routing](../validation/s5-5-context-routing.md).

## Work and Life Context

- GitHub, Slack, Google Drive and Teams reads converged on bounded `work.context` data with selected
  scopes, typed partial failure and no provider action authority.
- Gmail logistics candidates and selected Home Assistant state converged on `life.logistics`, while
  payment, security, access-code and high-authority home data remained excluded.
- Work Context and Life Logistics Experts were connected through enabled durable, person/source-
  scoped assignments.
- Evidence: [Work and Life Logistics foundation](../validation/s5-5-work-logistics-foundation.md).

## Native Personal Context

- Apple and Android sources published strict Calendar, People, Feasibility, Attention and derived
  Wellbeing Views through the local person/device/freshness boundary.
- Schedule, Relationships, Focus and Wellbeing consumed multi-View inputs while preserving typed
  unavailable states and source provenance.
- Evidence: [native context Expert](../validation/s5-5-native-context-expert.md),
  [personal context Views](../validation/s5-5-personal-context-views.md), and
  [personal domain Experts](../validation/s5-5-personal-domain-experts.md).

## Checkpoint Limits

- Automated Go race/vet, Rust conformance/FFI and focused Flutter/native checks passed for the
  increments recorded above.
- Live provider, signed physical-device, combined source-quality and integrated privacy/failure
  evidence remained open; none of these increments alone advanced an acceptance criterion.
