# 3-B — AppHost Composition Closure

**Status: pending · scope fixed from Stage 3 baseline review**

**Baseline used for scope:** `39263a1339d0519b78a20680c0eea73506ff6d6f`

## Goal

Make AppHost/App a pure construction, lifecycle/admission and typed owner-request routing boundary.

App may instantiate concrete repositories/adapters and inject them into owners. App must not interpret model credentials, choose model placement, authorize sources/grants/recipients, or duplicate owner policy.

3-B begins only after 3-A is complete/frozen.

## Composition rule

Allowed in App:

- construct concrete owner services;
- bind repositories, Keychain/native/server adapters and platform brokers;
- translate verified host caller identity into owner request identity;
- manage AppHost request lifetime/shutdown;
- map owner failures to product-service errors.

Not allowed in App:

- choose Foundation versus Server;
- calculate model route/recipient/consent;
- parse a bearer to make model/source policy decisions;
- decide which source/grant is authorized;
- duplicate retry/usage ownership;
- treat provider/source availability as business eligibility.

A direct `floe_provider_adapters` import is not automatically wrong. Construction/injection is App work; business judgment is not.

## Reviewed live seams

### B1 — Conversation credential-source seam

`crates/app/src/turn_request.rs` still carries private
`SavedConnectionSource` and `ConversationTurnRequest::stored_server_connection()`.

External production callers cannot choose the source, which was sufficient for Stage 2. The final App boundary should instead pass pure turn/runtime intent while concrete saved-connection stores are injected in the service graph.

Do not expose a new public credential handle to replace the private enum.

### B2 — per-turn provider/source preparation

`crates/app/src/vault_host/conversation_turn.rs` still has an explicit
`BOUNDARY(stage-3)` and prepares `ServerSourceClient` directly.

After 3-A, move this construction behind the narrow owner/provider port used by Context/Access. App may supply the store/adapter implementation but should not repeatedly read/interpret the connection to decide source behavior.

### B3 — inference/recipient concrete graph

`crates/app/src/inference_routes.rs` is already mostly construction-only. Do not delete it because of the word “routes”.

Converge its remaining responsibilities into one service graph where practical:

- current saved-connection store;
- root model provider;
- exact-recipient authority;
- remote source provider.

The same verified person/device binding must be preserved.

### B4 — AppWire query/events concrete access

The new Conversation command path uses `HostRequest` plus the
`ConversationCommands` trait.

`crates/bindings/ffi/src/app_wire.rs` query/events still reach
`host_request.services().agent_vault()` directly.

Add/use narrow typed App service interfaces for Conversation query/event access so FFI does not depend on concrete `AppComposition`/VaultBridge internals.

Do not move Conversation query semantics into FFI.

### B5 — `legacy_services()` caller migration preparation

Old FFI paths still use:

~~~text
FloeHandle.services()
  → AppHost::legacy_services()
  → concrete AppComposition
~~~

3-B should make canonical host-service access sufficient for real product callers. Do not delete `legacy_services()` until 3-C/3-D migrate callers; 3-E owns deletion after caller count is zero.

## Explicitly not a broad App rewrite

Reviewed `FloeCore` Day methods mostly delegate to `floe_day::DayService`, and the Actions facade leaves review/ledger/dispatch policy in `floe-actions` while providing concrete native/repository dependencies.

Do not relocate thin owner delegates solely to make App smaller.

Only move policy or topology that violates the composition rule above.

## Acceptance

3-B is complete when:

- AppHost admission/lifecycle and AppComposition construction are distinct from owner policy;
- `ConversationTurnRequest` is pure intent/runtime identity and does not own credential lookup semantics;
- App does not prepare/choose a model or source route as a business decision;
- Context/Access/Inference receive injected ports rather than App-derived policy;
- AppWire Conversation command/query/event paths use typed host/owner services rather than concrete VaultBridge internals;
- no grant/recipient authorization is re-derived in App;
- thin Day/Actions owner delegates are not churned without an ownership reason;
- deferred FFI compatibility remains only where a live 3-C/3-D caller requires it.

On completion, mark 3-B complete/frozen, update the Stage 3 checkpoint to 3-C and stop.
